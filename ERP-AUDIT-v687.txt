REPAIR

4. The same reconciliation runs after every incremental cloud update and is idempotent.
5. Opening loose non-gold is deducted karat-wise from Fine Gold, less active production material already represented by an explicit embedded source.
6. All corrections are written to the ERP audit ledger.
