KHUSHALI JEWELLS MANUFACTURING ERP - V678 AUDIT
Date: 05-10-2026

1. JOB CARD ITEM TRANSFER
- A selected PR item can be transferred from its current Job Card to any other existing Job Card.
- The destination list is searchable by Job Card, customer, category, status, PR, and design.
- A direct Transfer To Another Job Card button is available inside each item detail.
- PR number, design, weights, current department, transfer history, Bill link, and item status remain unchanged.
- Linked lot, Setting, Bill, Safe, department issue/return, non-gold, and factory references are synchronized by stable item ID.
- The transfer records operator, timestamp, source Job Card, destination Job Card, and PR numbers in the ERP ledger.
- Owner, Manager, and Order Dept have permission; Demo remains read only.

2. PERMANENT NON-GOLD SHELF
- Added a full-screen Non-Gold Shelf under Safe Locker.
- Separate Stone, Black Beads, Moti, Spring, and Other balances are shown by karat.
- Loose Shelf, Casting/Embedded Shelf, Department, WIP, PR/Item, Bill Pending, Total ERP, and unmatched weights are visible.
- Opening or purchased non-gold can be added to the loose shelf and issued through the existing Safe issue workflow.
- Casting wax stone is recognized as embedded stock and is not mixed with issueable loose shelf stock.
- Dashboard Non-Gold opens this shelf directly.

3. DATA AND CLOUD SAFETY
- No PR number is regenerated by an item transfer.
- No production weight is added, deducted, or duplicated by the transfer.
- Local save is completed before immediate Supabase synchronization is attempted.
- No Supabase schema change is required for V678.
