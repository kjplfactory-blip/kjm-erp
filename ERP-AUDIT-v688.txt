KHUSHALI JEWELLS MANUFACTURING ERP v688 AUDIT

PROBLEM CONFIRMED
1. Non-gold held in the shelf was already included in factory and department GW.
2. Treating that shelf as another physical GW location counted the same material twice.
3. Older transfer balances mixed net-weight and gross-weight rules, which made ordinary department holdings difficult to reconcile.

IMPLEMENTED MODEL
1. Central Non-Gold Shelf tracks Stone, Black Beads, Moti, Spring and Other by karat.
2. It is a composition register, not an additional physical-GW location.
3. Ordinary department movement and holding use GW only: Issue GW minus Receive GW.
4. Setting is the only exception: Receive Net equals Receive GW minus Hand Stone; Metal Balance equals Issue GW minus Receive Net.
5. Fine Sheet values physical GW at karat first, then deducts central non-gold once at the matching karat.
6. Existing transfer balances are reconciled automatically. Repeating the reconciliation does not change an already-correct record.
