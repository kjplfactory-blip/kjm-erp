KHUSHALI JEWELLS MANUFACTURING - v603 DASHBOARD DEPARTMENT TILES
Updated: 24 September 2026 IST

Each department tile now shows:
1. GW DIFF (ISSUE - RECEIVE) - the prominent current gross-weight holding.
2. NET WT DIFF - the current net gold weight after recorded non-gold is removed.
3. NON-GOLD HOLDING - wax stone + hand stone + moti/black beads/spring/other non-gold.
4. FINE GOLD HOLDING - current net gold converted by the recorded karat/purity.
5. HISTORY GW DIFF - the same department balance reconstructed from Transfer History.

The hover detail also shows:
- Issued To Department GW.
- Received From Department GW.
- History GW Diff.
- History Net Diff.

When current GW and History GW differ, the yellow HISTORY VARIANCE warning remains visible with the reason to recheck.
This is a display and verification update only. Existing stock, job cards, department transfers and cloud data are not changed.
