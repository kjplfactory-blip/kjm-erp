KHUSHALI JEWELLS MANUFACTURING ERP - V679 AUDIT
Date: 05-10-2026

1. MULTIPLE BILL FACTORY OUT
- Factory Out now shows a searchable list of every completed bill waiting to leave the factory.
- Users can select individual bills, Select All visible bills, or clear the selection.
- Combined bill count, item count, GW, non-gold, net weight, and fine weight are shown before posting.
- An optional common WSTG percentage can be applied to the selection; leaving it blank keeps each bill's saved value.

2. LEDGER AND DATA SAFETY
- Every selected bill creates its own normal Factory Ledger entry and remains independently traceable and reversible.
- Bills are validated together before saving; a local validation or storage failure rolls back the whole uncommitted batch.
- Already-posted bills are excluded and cannot be posted twice.
- The whole batch uses one local save, one Supabase write, and one cloud read-back instead of repeating cloud operations per bill.
- A cloud delay never removes the confirmed local batch; normal background sync continues retrying.

3. COMPATIBILITY
- Existing single-bill Factory Out and manual metal / stock Factory Out remain available.
- Destination remains KJPL Office for bill Factory Out.
- Existing ERP data is preserved and no Supabase SQL change is required.
