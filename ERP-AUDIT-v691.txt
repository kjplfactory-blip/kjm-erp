KHUSHALI JEWELLS MANUFACTURING ERP v691 AUDIT

REQUIREMENT
Every Bill posted through Factory Out must be visible as a Billing outward movement and must reduce Billing GW holding by the same posted Bill GW.

IMPLEMENTATION
1. Posted Factory Out Bills generate one derived, read-only Bill To Factory Out movement.
2. The movement originates from the same Billing department identity used by the lot's inward transfer.
3. Receive GW in Billing OUT equals the Bill final GW; Net Weight equals the Factory Out Bill net weight.
4. Bill number, Lot, Job Card, pieces and non-gold breakup remain traceable in the movement remarks.
5. Existing posted Bills appear automatically without adding stored duplicate transactions.
6. The movement is visible in Department Transfer History, Online Transfer History, Lot History and Bill Lot Movement Trace.
7. Reversal or deletion remains consistent because visibility is derived from the Factory Out posting marker.

VALIDATION
- JavaScript syntax check passed.
- Factory Out movements are keyed uniquely by Bill ID.
- Department OUT GW is sourced from the same Bill summary used by the Factory Ledger.
- Version and browser cache markers are aligned to v691.

DATA SAFETY
- Complete v690 source backup was created before editing.
- No synced ERP business data was modified during development.
- No reset and no Supabase SQL change are required.
