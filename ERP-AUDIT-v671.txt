KHUSHALI JEWELLS MANUFACTURING - ERP v671 AUDIT

SETTING SETTLEMENT
- A fresh Job Lot no longer creates an Issued/pending setter entry.
- Setting Master selects Lot and Setter, then enters Issue GW and Receive GW in one form.
- Hand Stone comes from the Job Card and is read-only except for Owner.
- Receive Net = Receive GW - Hand Stone.
- Final Setter Metal Balance = Issue GW - Receive Net - Returned Material - Loss.
- The Lot remains in Setting Master after the settlement is saved.
- The completed Setter Account History row retains Lot No and Job Card No.

COMPATIBILITY
- Old pending setter records can still be closed from Previous Pending Entries.
- Monthly Rawa/WSTG receipt and Setter Loss remain available.
- Existing v670 duplicated tags, red customer tags, day-close backup and bag traceability remain intact.
- No database schema change and no data reset.
