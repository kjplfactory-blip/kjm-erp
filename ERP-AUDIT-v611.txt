VERSION v611 - SETTING MANAGER NUMBERED SPLIT JOB CARDS
UPDATED: 24-09-2026 14:18 IST

REQUEST
- When Setting Manager splits a Job Card, issued PR items must leave the main Job Card.
- Split cards must use Job Card No-1, No-2, No-3, and so on.
- The last remaining lot must stay available as the original full Job Card.

IMPLEMENTED
1. Every partial setter issue creates a separate numbered Job Card.
2. The first split is Main Job Card-1, followed by -2, -3, and onward.
3. Selected PR items are reassigned to that split Job Card.
4. Selected items no longer appear in the original main Job Card.
5. Unselected PR items stay under the original Job Card number.
6. The last remaining PR lot can be issued through Full Job Lot.
7. Split lots, setter entries, and ledger references show the numbered Job Card.
8. Job Order search groups the numbered cards with their main Job Card.
9. Existing Merge Job Cards logic recognizes the Setting split family.
10. Earlier Setting partial splits are migrated in date order to the same numbering.
