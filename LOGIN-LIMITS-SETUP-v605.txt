SETTING MANAGER - TWO DEVICE LOGIN SETUP

1. Open Supabase for the live ERP project.
2. Open SQL Editor.
3. Open ENABLE-LOGIN-LIMITS.sql from this v605 folder.
4. Copy the complete SQL into a new query and click Run.
5. Confirm the result says LOGIN LIMITS READY.
6. Upload the complete v605 website folder to GitHub.
7. Open the refreshed website on both Setting Manager devices and log in normally.

EXPECTED LIMITS
- Owner: 2 devices.
- Manager: 2 devices.
- Setting Manager: 2 devices.
- Every other login: 1 device.

The SQL does not change ERP stock, Job Cards, bills, production, designs, or erp_state data.
