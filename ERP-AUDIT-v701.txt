ERP AUDIT v701 - JOB CARD FAMILY CONTROL

FAMILY CONTROL
- The main Job Card is the permanent family reference.
- Main and child cards are shown together in one full-screen Job Card window.
- Each child retains its Job Card number, PR items, active production lot, department, GW and transfer history.
- The family total is calculated from unique active physical lots.
- Each active lot has direct Transfer Exact Lot and History controls.
- Pending children remain separate and show Gold Not Issued.
- A child can be opened directly without losing the family overview.

HUMAN-ERROR PROTECTION
- A PR linked to more than one active lot is detected and shown as a grouped warning.
- A lot whose displayed Job Card does not match its linked PR items is detected.
- A Job Card family with an integrity warning cannot be split or merged again until checked.
- Cards in different departments, or a pending card and a live production card, cannot be physically merged.
- Multi-stage cards no longer show an ambiguous Mixed label; they show the number of active stages and the exact lot rows provide the actionable status.

DATA SAFETY
- No existing Job Card, Bill, lot, transfer, weight, design, library, customer, vendor or inventory record was changed by this upgrade.
- The v700 code folder and a complete live-cloud JSON copy were saved before implementation.
- No Supabase SQL or schema change is required.
