ACCOUNTING MODEL

FITTING
- Stone may be added directly in Fitting.
- Black Beads, Moti, Spring and Other must first be issued from Non-Gold Shelf to Fitting.
- A Fitting transfer assigns that already-issued material to the lot without changing GW or duplicating factory non-gold.
- New transfers are blocked if Fitting does not hold enough matching material and karat.
- Existing v698 Fitting entries keep their original shelf allocation method for historical accuracy.

SETTING

FACTORY OUT

- Bill reversal automatically reopens that department allocation.
