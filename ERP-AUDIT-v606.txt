KHUSHALI JEWELLS MANUFACTURING
ERP AUDIT - v606
Date: 2026-09-24 IST

CHANGE
Billing WIP can be issued fully or in multiple portions to Job Card items. A portion may start a new production movement or be added to an item that already has an active lot.

ACCOUNTING CONTROL
- Only the entered Issue GW is removed from the Billing holding.
- The remaining Billing GW stays selectable for a later issue.
- Source wax and saved non-gold are allocated proportionately from the source holding.
- Job Card wax and hand stone can be checked or edited for each portion.
- Net Gold is Issue GW less wax, hand stone and any carried non-gold.
- The final portion closes the Billing source automatically.

TRACEABILITY
- Every part issue receives a new lot number.
- The child lot keeps the original Job Card, PR number, source holding and active-lot reference.
- Online Transfer History shows Billing to destination.
- Department Transfer History shows one Billing OUT and one destination IN entry.
- The ledger records issued GW, wax, hand, total non-gold, net gold, remaining Billing balance and active-lot reference.

COMPATIBILITY
Existing v604/v605 Billing-to-Job-Card records remain readable. No database schema or Supabase SQL change is required.
