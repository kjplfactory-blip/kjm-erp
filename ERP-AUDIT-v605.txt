KHUSHALI JEWELLS MANUFACTURING - v605 SETTING MANAGER LOGIN LIMIT
Updated: 24 September 2026 IST

SIMULTANEOUS LOGIN POLICY
- Owner: maximum 2 active devices.
- Manager: maximum 2 active devices.
- Setting Manager: maximum 2 active devices.
- Every other built-in or custom login: maximum 1 active device.
- A disconnected login slot expires after 15 minutes when its heartbeat stops.

WEBSITE CONTROL
- The login page now displays the revised policy.
- Owner Login Details reports 2 active devices for Setting Manager.
- The Setting Manager built-in ID is recognised case-insensitively.
- The saved-login policy version was increased so existing sessions are revalidated.

SUPABASE CONTROL
- ENABLE-LOGIN-LIMITS.sql now grants 2 slots when the login ID is Setting Manager or the saved role is setting-manager.
- The database function keeps the atomic lock and device-count check, preventing two laptops from taking the same final slot simultaneously.
- Run ENABLE-LOGIN-LIMITS.sql once in Supabase SQL Editor and confirm LOGIN LIMITS READY.

DATA SAFETY
- The SQL updates only the login-session table and its three session functions.
- ERP job cards, production, stock, designs, bills and erp_state are not read, reset or modified.
