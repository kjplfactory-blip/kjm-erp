KHUSHALI JEWELLS MANUFACTURING ERP v686 AUDIT

PROBLEM CONFIRMED
- Legacy WIP/Shelf rows contain the physical product GW but often have no saved wax-stone composition.
- Issue Gold calculated the lot as GW minus Job Card Wax Stone, but removed only Net Gold from the source row.
- The unremoved Wax amount remained in the source and appeared again in Production, duplicating stock and fine calculations.

CORRECTION
1. Issue Gold validates and removes the full entered GW from the selected source.
2. Net Gold is GW minus the Job Card Wax Stone.
3. Missing source composition is allocated from the matching karat Loose Non-Gold Stone balance.
4. The lot stores source provenance so Bill Factory Out credits the embedded stone and does not deduct it twice.
5. Sources already carrying wax retain that composition and do not consume Loose Stone again.
6. Edit Issue and Delete Issue reverse the exact source GW and Loose Stone rows.
7. Active legacy net-only issues are repaired automatically only when the source snapshots and outgoing rows match the defect exactly.
8. A shortage blocks the transaction without changing any stock.

EXPECTED MOVEMENT EXAMPLE
- Issue GW: 35.450 g
- Wax Stone already inside GW: 8.636 g
- Net Gold transferred: 26.814 g
- WIP/Shelf source reduction: 35.450 g
- Loose Non-Gold Stone reduction: 8.636 g
- Production holding: GW 35.450 g / Stone 8.636 g / Net Gold 26.814 g

DATA SAFETY
- No reset is performed.
- No Supabase SQL change is required.
- Existing Job Cards, Bills, designs, stone library and moti library are preserved.
