KHUSHALI JEWELLS MANUFACTURING - v604 BILLING TO JOB CARD ISSUE
Updated: 24 September 2026 IST

NEW BILLING ACTION
- Open BILL and select ISSUE BILLING ITEM / WIP.
- Select a Billing-held WIP item.
- Choose ISSUE BILLING ITEM TO JOB CARD.
- Select the pending Job Card item and its current / starting department.

AUTOMATIC WEIGHT LOGIC
- Issue GW remains the complete Billing holding transferred to the Job Card.
- Wax Stone is read from the selected Job Card item.
- Hand Stone is read from the selected Job Card item.
- Total Non-Gold = Wax Stone + Hand Stone + any other non-gold already recorded with the Billing item.
- Net Gold = Issue GW - Total Non-Gold.
- Example: 100.000 g GW - 2.300 g Wax Stone - 3.400 g Hand Stone = 94.300 g Net Gold.

STOCK AND HISTORY CONTROL
- The Job Card's wax and hand-stone weight is adjusted through the existing main karat-wise non-gold stock allocation.
- The Billing source holding closes only after the replacement Job Card lot is saved successfully.
- Total factory GW does not increase or decrease during this internal movement.
- Online Transfer History shows Billing to the selected Job Card department.
- Department Transfer History records Billing OUT and destination department IN exactly once.

PERMISSIONS
- Bill Department, Order Department, Manager and Owner can issue eligible Billing WIP to a Job Card.
- Only pending Job Card items without another production lot are available, preventing duplicate production holdings.

DATA SAFETY
- A failed validation or local save restores the previous order, lot, source holding, counters and ledger.
- Existing Job Cards, production, stock, bills, designs and Supabase data are not reset.
- No Supabase SQL change is required.
