SCOPE
- Setter return difference and cumulative setter metal balance.

SETTER ACCOUNT FLOW
- A returned lot is marked Received even when Issue GW and Receive Net do not balance to zero.
- The remaining difference stays against that setter as a returned-lot metal balance.
- Active lots still physically with a setter remain separate from returned-lot metal due.
- Setter tiles and the Setting summary display both values.
- Monthly rawa or loss is entered against one Combined Setter Balance.
- The combined amount is allocated across the setter's original returned lots and each allocation remains visible in its settlement history.
- Setter loss allocations continue to appear in the unified Loss Ledger.

STONE FLOW
- Additional Stone uses Hand Setting and the Setting manufacturing stage.
- Cumulative Hand Stone equals all Hand Stone rows plus Additional Stone weight.
- Saving a PR stone plan updates the live Setting lot and current setter entry immediately.
- If a lot was already received but remains in Setting, its Receive Net and outstanding setter balance are recalculated from the updated hand-stone total.
- Once the PR item plan is updated, Stone Master and Design Master cannot replace it; current rows remain editable and new rows can be added.
- Design Master remains unchanged by Job Card item edits.

VERIFIED VALUES
- Issue 10.000 g, Receive GW 9.700 g, Hand Stone 0.200 g => Receive Net 9.500 g and Setter Balance 0.500 g.
- Two returned lots with combined balance 1.000 g accepted 0.700 g WSTG/Rawa and 0.300 g loss => Balance 0.000 g.
- Hand row 0.400 g plus Additional Stone 0.100 g => cumulative Hand Stone 0.500 g.
