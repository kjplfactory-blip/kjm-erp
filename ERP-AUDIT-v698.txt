ERP AUDIT v698 - FITTING NON-GOLD SHELF CONSUMPTION

REQUIREMENT
- Non-gold embedded during Fitting must not create duplicate stock.
- Black Beads, Moti, Spring and Other must come from the matching karat Loose Non-Gold Shelf.
- Stone is the direct production exception.

ACCOUNTING RESULT
- Non-stone loose shelf decreases by the entered weight.
- The same weight is assigned to the production lot.
- Total factory non-gold remains unchanged while the item stays inside the factory.
- Factory Out removes the lot-linked amount once.
- Transfer edit reverses the old allocation before applying the replacement.
- Transfer delete restores the consumed loose shelf stock.

DATA SAFETY
- v697 entries are migrated only when matching shelf stock is available.
- A shortage blocks a new transfer without saving partial changes.
- No Supabase schema change is required.
