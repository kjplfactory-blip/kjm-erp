CUSTOMER ORDER TAG COLOUR AUDIT

Scope
- Bill item tag printing on A4 (40 tags) and A6 (10 tags).
- Single Bill tag printing and combined multi-Bill tag printing.

Verified behaviour
- Customer order is detected by the existing manufacturing order rule: every customer except KJPL-STOCK.
- Each customer-order tag receives the customer-order-tag class independently.
- Customer-order tag content prints with a light pink fill and pink border.
- KJPL-STOCK tag content remains white.
- The barcode area stays white with black barcode lines for scanning contrast.
- Print colour adjustment is enabled so supported browsers retain the customer colour.

Data safety
- No ERP business data is migrated, cleared or rewritten.
- No Supabase schema change is required.
- Existing permissions, Bill values and tag-printed status logic are unchanged.
