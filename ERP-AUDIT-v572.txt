VERIFIED DAILY TALLY AND LOSS
- Selecting a department filters Department Tally History and Loss Ledger to that department.
- A saved positive tally loss creates one linked department loss record.
- The linked loss reduces department GW, factory GW, net gold and fine gold once.
- Karat-specific tallies use the selected karat; all-karat tallies use the department's live purity split.
- Editing replaces the linked loss instead of duplicating it.
- Deleting the tally deletes the linked loss and restores the stock.
- Test: 0.500 g loss reduced department and factory GW by 0.500 g and restored exactly after removal.
