KHUSHALI JEWELLS MANUFACTURING ERP
VERSION v633 AUDIT

CORRECTION
- The 0.004 g Bill allowance is applied during the final successful Bill save.
- It is applied once to every Bill item having a positive Final GW.
- Net weight and fine weight are recalculated immediately from the adjusted GW.

REPEAT PROTECTION
- Each adjusted item stores its stable PR/order key in the Bill record.
- Per-item adjustment fields and Bill-level keys survive state normalization and cloud reload.
- Reopening, editing, QC processing, printing, or repeatedly saving the same Bill cannot apply the allowance again.
- A later manually edited Final GW is respected and is not automatically increased again.

COMPATIBILITY
- Existing Bills already carrying v632 item adjustment markers remain protected.
- The v632 stone auto-sync and Refresh Stone Entry behavior is retained.
- No Supabase schema change is required.
