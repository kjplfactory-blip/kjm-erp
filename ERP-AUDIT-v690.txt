KHUSHALI JEWELLS MANUFACTURING ERP v690 AUDIT

REQUIREMENT
1. A vendor Factory In may be a rejected jewellery item containing Stone, Black Beads, Moti, Spring and Other non-gold material.
2. Casting Item and Rod must remain separate categories throughout the ERP.

IMPLEMENTATION
1. Factory In has a Rejected Item With Non-Gold material type and separate component-weight fields.
2. The screen calculates Total Non-Gold and Net Gold before save and blocks non-gold above GW.
3. The rejected item enters the appropriate karat Safe Locker at full GW with its complete component breakup.
4. Vendor and Factory Fine calculations use rejected-item Net Gold, while the embedded non-gold is recorded as classification/audit data and not duplicated as another physical holding.
5. Factory ledger, Fine Sheet and edit screens display GW, component breakup, Total Non-Gold and Net Gold.
6. Casting Item and Rod have separate types and balances in Factory In/Out, Safe Locker, melting receipt and department receipt.
7. Existing ambiguous records are classified from saved source line, description and linked shelf record. No weight is changed.

VALIDATION
- JavaScript syntax check passed.
- Factory rejected-item form IDs are unique.
- No combined Casting Item / Rod labels remain in the active program files.
- Version and cache markers are aligned to v690.

DATA SAFETY
- Complete v689 source backup was created before editing.
- No synced ERP business data was modified during development.
- No reset and no Supabase SQL change are required.
